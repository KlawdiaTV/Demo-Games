THE PARLOR — DEMO REVIEWS

My first impressions, personal experiences & thoughts on each demo.

Terrible Lizards
Interesting

Terrible Lizards was a surprisingly interesting demo! I really enjoyed the gameplay, especially the variety of tasks and puzzles you have to complete while trying to survive. The dinosaurs chasing you are absolutely terrifying, and the parkour mechanics made the experience even more exciting. I loved having to move around, figure things out, and occasionally run for my life! However, there's one thing I wasn't a big fan of: the amount of reading. There are so many notes and documents scattered around, and some contain way too much text. I understand they're there to explain the story, but honestly, who's going to stop and read an entire novel when there are dinosaurs trying to eat you? I would have preferred the story to be delivered in a more engaging way rather than through so many lengthy documents. Despite that, I had a great time with the demo. The gameplay was fun, the puzzles were interesting, and the combination of dinosaurs, exploration, and parkour worked really well. Final impression: Interesting. An entertaining and promising demo with some great gameplay mechanics. I'm definitely interested in seeing more, and there's a good chance I'll give the full game a try someday!

Forsaken Fleet
Interesting

Forsaken Fleet was a really interesting horror experience, but apparently my trauma from The Lightkeeper wasn't enough, because THE CROWS AND SEAGULLS ARE BACK! Seriously, why do these games keep finding me? What did I ever do to these birds?! The game does a great job of combining a creepy atmosphere with some well-placed jumpscares and that constant feeling that something terrible is about to happen. Even the smallest sounds made me nervous, and I found myself getting scared by things that probably weren't even dangerous. And then there are the creatures chasing you. Absolutely terrifying! Small spoiler ahead: Apparently, being stranded in a creepy place wasn't bad enough. Now we have zombie-like pirates wandering around, looking for their next meal. And guess who's on the menu? ME, OF COURSE! I really liked how the game builds tension through its environment rather than relying entirely on jumpscares. The atmosphere keeps you uncomfortable, and when something finally happens, it hits even harder. Final impression: Interesting. A creepy and entertaining horror demo with some great scares, terrifying creatures, and an atmosphere that keeps you constantly on edge. I'm interested in seeing what the full game has to offer, although I'd really appreciate it if the birds could leave me alone for once!

Scawy Game
Really Enjoyed It

FINALLY! A horror game that starts during the day! I was beginning to think horror developers had forgotten the sun existed. Of course, the scary stuff still happens at night, because apparently we're not allowed to feel safe for too long. Scawy Game was such a fun experience! It had a little bit of everything: jumpscares that actually got me, ridiculously funny moments, weird characters, ghosts, possessed people, and, of course, THE ALMIGHTY BASEMENT! Because what would a horror game be without a creepy basement where absolutely nothing good can happen? What I loved most was how the game managed to mix horror with humor. One moment I was laughing at something completely ridiculous, and the next I was questioning every decision that had brought me there. The combination of creepy situations, bizarre characters, and unexpected funny moments made the whole experience incredibly entertaining. It wasn't just about being scared; it was about having fun while being scared! Final impression: Really Enjoyed It. A fantastic mix of horror, humor, weirdness, and chaos. I had an amazing time with this demo, and I'm definitely playing the full game when I get the chance!
